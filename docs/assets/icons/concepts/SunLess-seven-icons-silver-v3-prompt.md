# SunLess — серебряные иконки, версия 3

## Базовый запрос

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE new, visually distinct icon collage for SunLess, a dark narrative tarot game. EXACTLY SEVEN hand-engraved emblem icons with seven correct Cyrillic captions. This is an ALTERNATIVE design to a modern square vector UI: it must look like tiny antique etched wax seals or tarnished steel coin impressions from a grim old card deck. No square tiles, no glossy vector logos, no large white-filled symbols.

LAYOUT: refined landscape 16:9 sheet on near-black matte paper #0E0F14. Exactly 4 circular emblems in the upper row and 3 circular emblems centered in the lower row; generous even spacing. Each emblem same diameter and optical weight, enclosed by a thin imperfect moon-silver circular line and faint inner hatching. Under each emblem is one small flat dark parchment caption tab. Read left-to-right:
upper row: СИЛА, ВОЛЯ, ХИТРОСТЬ, МОНСТР.
lower row: УСИЛЕНИЕ, ПЕРСОНАЖ, СЮЖЕТ.
These seven words are the ONLY text anywhere; exact spelling, uppercase Cyrillic, no extra letters. Every emblem and caption fits completely inside the image.

SEVEN NEW EMBLEMS — radically different imagery from stock UI icons:
1 СИЛА: a compact armored fist gripping a snapped iron chain link; exactly one fist and chain, silhouette still readable small.
2 ВОЛЯ: a single thin candle whose flame burns upright inside a broken stone ring; quiet endurance, no hand or torch.
3 ХИТРОСТЬ: one narrow watchful eye partially concealed behind two diagonal shadow cuts, not a radiant mystical eye.
4 МОНСТР: three deep claw marks tearing through a small irregular dark shield, no animal face, horns or skull.
5 УСИЛЕНИЕ: two overlapping miniature tarot cards, foremost with a tiny four-point star inset; no gemstone and no up-arrow.
6 ПЕРСОНАЖ: side-profile cameo of a cloaked human head and shoulders inside the coin, identifiable as human; no blank bust pictogram.
7 СЮЖЕТ: an open book whose center fold forms a single winding path toward a tiny distant point, subtle bronze-gold only on the page edge.

STYLE: fine old woodcut / copperplate engraving with sparse hand-hatched silver lines, slightly weathered and uneven but carefully drawn, matte charcoal paper, restrained ivory labels in classical Cyrillic serif. Dull silver #A9AFB9 is dominant, lower than pure white; monster has only a muted oxblood cut #6E1E26, story has minimal antique gold #B89A5E. Important: the icons must remain legible at 64 pixels without thick outlines. Every mark should feel carved into paper/metal. Low-key dark exposure, no bright fill, no glow, no gradient, no excessive decorative rays.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS: no square cards or rectangular tiles, no modern flat vector style, no photorealistic characters, no scenic illustration, no extra objects or duplicate symbols, no eighth item, no title at top, no invented runes, no English, no pseudo-text, no watermarks, no crop.
